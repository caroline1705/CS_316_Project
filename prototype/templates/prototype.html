Data & model notes

Demo rate date: November 4, 2026. Results are calculated by the local server from the seeded database. FPL: 2026 team seed. Medical mileage: latest seeded rate on snapshot date. Lodging: FY2027. Childcare: latest seeded DOL study year (2022 dollars, not inflation-adjusted). Treatment course lengths and visits come from the existing SQL query.

Travel = visits × round-trip miles × medical mileage allowance (a proxy, not fuel receipts). One hotel night plus meals per visit at 100+ one-way miles. Childcare = visits × children × weekly county price ÷ 5. General living expenses reduce available income; they are not added again to treatment costs. No geocoding, parking, tolls, companion travel, clinical stage verification, insurance network lookup, or benefit-year resets.

Assistance entries retain source and application links from the repository. Fund availability, free-text requirements, and documentation need manual verification, including entries marked closed in their notes. Unknown subtype remains a potential match. A failed explicit rule is shown under exclusions.
